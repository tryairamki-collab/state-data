Macroeconomic Trend Correlation Guide

1. Consumption vs Asset Creation Multipliers: Welfare spending directly injects liquidity into local household budgets, providing a short-term velocity shift to private retail consumption. However, this revenue-heavy allocation carries a low multiplier value (~0.90x to 1.20x), often compressing capital outlay funds required to build persistent logistics capacity which scales multi-year GSDP.

2. Debt-Servicing Traps: When direct-to-consumer populist payouts run ahead of standalone tax collection capacity, states fill budget deficits through massive open market bonds. As the debt-to-GSDP curve breaches structural thresholds (typically 25-30%), an expanding slice of revenue inflows is consumed strictly by interest liabilities, driving down fiscal space for industrial subsidies.
